Curiosity, carried

I’m a physics master’s student at Université Paris-Saclay, with research experience spanning exoplanets, Fast Radio Bursts, cosmic rays, globular clusters and cosmology.

Alongside research, I contribute to student-led physics through leadership, conference organisation and science outreach. I’m interested in the ideas that connect fundamental physics, the people who pursue them, and the communities that make science more accessible.

02RESEARCH

Questions at
different scales.

From transient radio signals to the dynamics of stellar systems, these are the research projects and internships that have shaped my scientific path.

01 / 06APR — AUG 2026

Characterisation of Exoplanets

Institut d’Astrophysique de Paris (IAP) · Paris, France

Characterising exoplanets with radial-velocity and transit methods. Working with SOPHIE, TESS and Kepler data for reduction and analysis, alongside five night-time observing missions at the Observatoire de Haute-Provence.

Prof. Guillaume Hébrard · Dr. Neda Heidari

ExoplanetsRadial velocityTransit photometrySOPHIE · TESS · Kepler
02 / 06JAN — JUL 2025

Cosmic Rays from Galactic Stellar Clusters

IJCLab, Université Paris-Saclay · Orsay, France

A multiwavelength study of cosmic rays and their spectral origin in stellar clusters and microquasars, using Markov Chain Monte Carlo methods to constrain radiative-model parameters.

Prof. Tiina Suomijärvi · Dr. Coline Dubos

Cosmic raysStellar clustersMicroquasarsMCMC
03 / 06JUN 2024 — PRESENT

Fast Radio Bursts & Cosmological Parameters

DS Kothari Centre, Miranda House · University of Delhi, India

Using localized Fast Radio Burst data to constrain the Hubble constant and other cosmological parameters through Markov Chain Monte Carlo methods, with attention to the dispersion measure of the host galaxy. A poster was presented at the end of the internship.

Dr. Abha Dev Habib · Dr. Nisha Rani

FRB cosmologyHubble constantHost-galaxy DMMCMC
04 / 06JUN 2023 — JUL 2024

Globular Cluster NGC 5466

DS Kothari Centre, Miranda House · University of Delhi, India

Studying the extra-tidal region of NGC 5466 and potential runaway stars, including the forces acting on the cluster through analysis of stellar density plots. A poster was presented at the end of the internship.

Dr. Richa Kundu

Globular clustersNGC 5466Extra-tidal regionStellar dynamics
05 / 06JUN — NOV 2022

Statistical Methods in Cosmology

DS Kothari Centre, Miranda House · University of Delhi, India

Exploring statistical methods for estimating cosmological parameters, including Monte Carlo methods, maximum likelihood, chi-square fitting and Markov Chain Monte Carlo. A poster was presented at the end of the internship.

Dr. Abha Dev Habib · Dr. Nisha Rani

CosmologyStatisticsMaximum likelihoodMCMC
06 / 06JUN — AUG 2021

QuarkNet: Atmospheric Effects on Muon Flux

DS Kothari Centre, Miranda House · University of Delhi, India

Investigating how time of day, temperature and barometric pressure affect the measured flux of muons.

Dr. S. N. Sandhya · Dr. Raishma Krishnan

Muon fluxQuarkNetExperimental physics
